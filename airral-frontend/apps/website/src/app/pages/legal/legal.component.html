Terms of service Privacy policy Cookie policy

Who we are

Airral is a job search service at airral.com, apply.airral.com and app.airral.com, and a Cictus product. "We" below means Airral. Questions or requests about your data: support@airral.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and your Google account ID.
  • Your resume: the file you upload, and what we read from it, such as skills, work history and education.
  • Your job search: jobs you save, where each one is in your tracker, your notes, and the match results we produce for your resume against a job.
  • Employer accounts: company name and web domain, and the jobs you post.
  • How the site is used: the pages you visit, the site you came from, and clicks on "Apply now". If you are signed in, these are linked to your account. To count visitors we keep a code made by scrambling your network address and browser together with the date. It changes every day, and we do not store the address itself.
  • Email updates: your address, if you ask us to keep you posted.
  • Google Analytics: see the cookie policy.

How we use it

  • To run the service: find jobs, score your resume against them, and keep your tracker.
  • To send account emails: confirming your address and resetting your password.
  • To keep accounts safe, for example by limiting repeated sign-in attempts.
  • To understand which parts of Airral help people, so we can improve them.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show ads.

Who else handles it

  • Google Cloud hosts Airral, its database and uploaded resumes, in the United States.
  • Google Firebase Authentication sends our verification and password-reset emails, and confirms you opened them.
  • Google Analytics measures traffic to our websites.

Jobs on Airral link to employers' own sites. When you click "Apply now" you leave Airral, and that employer's privacy policy covers what you send them.

How long we keep it

Account, resume and job-search data stay while your account is open. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months. To have your account and everything in it deleted, email support@airral.com from the address on the account.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing us.
  • Turn off job-alert emails in your settings, or with the link in any alert.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics as described in the cookie policy.

Children

Airral is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell account holders by email about anything significant.

Needed to work

When you sign in, your browser stores your session so you stay signed in. While you verify your email or reset your password, it briefly remembers the address you typed. This is browser storage rather than cookies. Your session is removed when you sign out.

Our own visitor count

We count visits without cookies. Each visit sends the page and the referring site to our servers, with a code that changes every day and cannot be used to follow you from one day to the next.

Google Analytics

On airral.com and apply.airral.com, Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_* to count visitors and to see where they come from and what devices they use. We send it only the page address, without search terms or anything after it, and we never load it on the email-verification, password-reset or unsubscribe pages. Google signals and ad personalisation are off. Google's own use of this data is described in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Turning Google Analytics off

  • Turn on Global Privacy Control or "Do Not Track" in your browser, and Airral will not load Google Analytics at all.
  • Or install Google's opt-out browser add-on.
  • Clearing or blocking cookies for airral.com also works, and the site still works without them.
