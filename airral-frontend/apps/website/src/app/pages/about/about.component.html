About Airral

We got tired
of job boards.

Airral is a hiring product with two halves. One does the looking for people who want a job. The other keeps a company's hiring in one place instead of six spreadsheets.

What we're building

Both sides of the same conversation.

Applying for work and hiring for work are the same event seen from two chairs, so we build them as one product. A candidate tells Airral what they are after once; it keeps watching, and reworks their résumé for the roles worth applying to. A company gets the applications, interviews, feedback and offers for a role in a single view.

We are early. Some of this is live today, some of it is still being built — including opening Airral up so assistants like Claude can search it directly.
